## What WalliD does
WalliD builds digital trust infrastructure for organisations: identity
wallets, verifiable credentials and signatures that integrate into existing
workflows (API-first, SDKs, cloud-native).

- **Digital identity wallets** — user-controlled, eIDAS-compliant wallets;
  organisations verify credentials instantly. Directly relevant to the EUDI
  Wallet ecosystem (PID / (Q)EAA, OpenID4VC, SD-JWT, mDL).
- **Credential issuer platform** — issue, manage and verify digital
  certificates / verifiable credentials (education, professional bodies,
  certification bodies).
- **Qualified electronic signatures** — QES with eID integration, EU-compliant.
- **Certified AI dataset marketplace** — provenance and IP protection for AI
  training data.

Positioning themes: eIDAS 2 / EUDI compliance by design, protection against
AI impersonation and deepfake fraud, gradual integration with legacy systems.
